1. What Information Do We Collect?

In Short: We collect journal entries and birth chart data for anonymous cloud backup, plus advertising identifiers via Google AdMob.

Personal information you provide to us

MyHoroscope does not require an account. If you use the "Secure Cloud Backup" feature, the App stores your journal entries, mood tracking, and birth chart information anonymously in the cloud. This data is not linked to your name, email, or any other identifier that identifies you as an individual. If you contact us voluntarily by email, we process your name and email address solely to respond to your inquiry.

Birth chart data

If you use the birth chart and natal insights features, the App collects your birth date, birth time, and birth place to calculate your chart. This information is stored anonymously as part of your cloud backup and is not linked to your identity.

Sensitive information

We do not process sensitive information in the sense of data that identifies racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, or the like. Your journal entries and mood tracking may contain personal reflections, but they are stored anonymously and are not accessible to us on an identified basis.

Purchase information

If you purchase a premium upgrade, the transaction is processed by Google Play Billing. We receive limited purchase/transaction history to manage your entitlement. We do not receive or store your payment card details; those are handled by Google Play.

Advertising data

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob collects your advertising ID and device/usage signals to serve and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Google settings.

Information collected automatically (distribution)

Google Play may collect standard distribution and crash information (e.g., install counts, device model, OS version) as part of delivering the App to you; this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not collect location, contacts, or any data beyond what's described above. We do not sell your data to third parties.

11. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature. No uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized, so we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings regardless of DNT.

Global Privacy Control (GPC): We recognize and honor GPC signals where applicable under US state privacy laws. When we detect a GPC signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.
