1. What Information Do We Collect?

In Short: We collect your Google account profile (name, email) and the content you create (favorites, custom cards, preferences, schedules) for cloud sync. We display ads via Google AdMob.

Personal information you provide to us

If you sign in with Google (Jetpack Credential Manager), we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • A unique account identifier (Google ID subject)
  • Your profile picture (optional, as shared by Google)

This information is stored in Firebase Firestore and linked to your account so your data syncs across devices. Signing in is optional — you can use the App offline without signing in, but your data will not sync across devices.

Content you create

When you use the App, you may create and we store (in Firestore, synced to your account):

  • Offline favorites (saved quotes and custom cards)
  • Custom cards created in the Personalization Studio (card text, selected background design, font choice, font size, text alignment, ink color, signature attribution)
  • Reading preferences (theme: Auto, Daylight, or Velvet Dark)
  • Scheduled message deliveries (scheduled dates, recurring rules, delivery history, status)
  • Partner name (if you personalize quotes with your partner's name)

Sensitive information

We do not process sensitive information in the sense of data that identifies racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, or the like. Your romantic messages and partner personalization are personal but not sensitive under applicable law. We do not access the content of messages you share via Android's share sheet to external apps (WhatsApp, SMS, etc.) — that sharing happens entirely outside our Services.

Advertising data

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob collects your advertising ID and device/usage signals to serve and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Google settings.

Information collected automatically (distribution)

Google Play may collect standard distribution and crash information (e.g., install counts, device model, OS version) as part of delivering the App to you; this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not collect location, contacts, or any data beyond what's described above. We do not sell your data to third parties.

8. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

In Short: We aim to protect your personal information through organizational and technical security measures.

  • Your data is encrypted in transit between the App and Firebase Firestore.
  • Each account's data is isolated at the database level — your favorites, custom cards, and schedules are not accessible to other users.
  • Firebase Firestore's offline persistence stores a local encrypted copy on your device so your data is available offline.
  • For the limited information processed for ad serving, we rely on Google AdMob's security measures.

Despite our safeguards, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. Transmission of personal information to and from our Services is at your own risk.

11. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature. No uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized, so we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings regardless of DNT.

Global Privacy Control (GPC): We recognize and honor GPC signals where applicable under US state privacy laws. When we detect a GPC signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.
