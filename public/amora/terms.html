2. Description of Service

Amora: Daily Love Quotes is a mobile application for sharing romantic messages with your partner. The App provides:

  • Daily Romantic Quotes with a deterministic Quote of the Day, personalized with your partner's name, and quick copy/send via Android's share sheet
  • A "Ready to Send" queue for due messages and milestone shortcuts
  • Relationship milestone categories: 48 curated quotes across 8 categories (Good Morning, Sweet Dreams, Anniversary, First Spark, Long Distance, Apology & Grace, Just Because, Wedding & Vows), plus My Creations and Offline Favorites
  • Personalization Studio: interactive card editor with live preview, 9 background designs (3 editorial artworks and 6 gradient stationery designs), 5 bundled Google Fonts (Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, Dancing Script, Lora, Plus Jakarta Sans), font size slider, text alignment, ink color swatches, and signature attribution
  • Automated message scheduling (Schedule): schedule one-time, daily, or weekly romantic messages and a daily quote reminder using AlarmManager and rich notifications with a one-tap Send Now action, plus delivery history tracking
  • Comprehensive Search: real-time search across all quotes, past sent messages (with send counts and timestamps), saved favorites, custom creations, and scheduled deliveries with category/status filter chips
  • Dark Mode: switch between Auto, Daylight, and low-glare Velvet Dark reading mode, persisted locally with DataStore and synced to your profile
  • Cloud Sync, Offline Access & Google Sign-In: authenticated via Google Sign-In (Jetpack Credential Manager) and backed by Firebase Firestore with offline persistence so saved favorites, custom cards, preferences, and schedules stay available offline and sync across devices
