2. Description of Service

InvoiceFlow is an offline-first invoicing and business document tool. The App provides:

  • Create invoices, receipts, and estimates offline
  • Business details and logo setup
  • Client and item directories, reusable on new documents
  • Calculate discounts, tax, amounts received, and outstanding balances
  • Manually record payments and view overdue or partly paid invoices
  • Convert estimates to invoices and search documents by number or client
  • Design flyers and brochures from selected products or services using built-in templates
  • Print documents or save them as PDFs using Android's print dialog
  • Export a backup file and restore it from Android's document picker
  • Sound and vibration settings

The App is free to use, works offline, displays no advertisements, includes no tracking or analytics, and requires no cloud account. InvoiceFlow stores your records on your device, creates ordinary business documents, and offers manual payment tracking.
