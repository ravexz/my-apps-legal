2. Description of Service

DairyPulse is an offline-first dairy herd management and milk production tracking solution for dairy farmers, herd managers, and veterinarians. The App provides:

  • Herd & cattle management: detailed animal profiles (ear tag/RFID, breed, birth date, lactation stage); animal status tracking (lactating, dry, heifer, pregnant, sick, sold, culled); historical production, health, and feeding logs per animal
  • Milk yield & production analytics: fast-entry milking session logging (morning, afternoon, evening); interactive analytics including daily herd yield trends, lactation curves, and period filters (7-day, 30-day, 90-day, 1-year); breed and herd productivity measurement over time
  • Veterinary health & alerts: record health interventions, vaccinations, treatments, follow-up dates, and withdrawal periods; automated vet alerts (Critical, Warning, Info) for active medical conditions; feed ration tracking (concentrates, silage, pasture) and feed efficiency ratio monitoring
  • Secure & offline resilient: 100% offline capability for logging data anywhere in the field without internet connectivity; military-grade AES-256 database encryption (SQLCipher) protects your farm and financial records; auto-queuing synchronization engine updates records when connectivity is restored
  • Multi-role staff management: support for farm owners, managers, veterinarians, and milkers with role-specific access
