2. Description of Service

OfficeTab is a mobile application for office lunch ladies (and similar food service staff) to track sales, orders, and payments. The App provides:

  • Sales tracking: record daily sales with items, quantities, unit prices, and totals
  • Order management: track orders with status and associated customer names (if you choose to record them)
  • Payment recording: log payment method (cash, mobile money) and amount for tracking purposes only — the App does not process payments
  • Cloud sync and offline access via Google Sign-In and Firebase Firestore, so your records stay available offline and sync across devices
  • Daily, weekly, and monthly sales summaries and basic reports

The App is free to use, with an optional premium upgrade that removes ads and unlocks additional reporting features.
