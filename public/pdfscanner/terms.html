PDF Scanner turns your phone into a document scanner. The App provides:

  • Scan & clean up: auto-detect document edges, crop, enhance contrast, remove shadows, and combine multi-page scans into a single PDF

  • More tools: image-to-text (OCR), ID card and passport templates, export pages as JPG, convert PDFs to images
