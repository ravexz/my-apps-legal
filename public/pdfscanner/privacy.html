1. What Information Do We Collect?

In Short: We collect information you provide (account email), document files if you enable cloud sync, purchase history, and advertising/device identifiers.

Information you provide to us

If you create an account (email/password or Google sign-in), we collect:

  • Your email address
  • An authentication identifier (managed by Supabase Auth)
  • If you enable cloud sync (off by default): your scanned pages and generated PDFs uploaded to your private, per-account cloud storage

Creating an account is optional — the App's scanning, editing, OCR, and annotation tools work without signing in. Documents created without an account are stored only in the App's private storage on your device.

Purchase information

If you purchase the Pro upgrade (one-time or subscription), the transaction is processed by RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. RevenueCat receives your purchase/transaction history and, if you're signed in, links it to your account identifier so your entitlement is preserved across devices. We do not receive or store your payment card details; those are handled by Google Play.

Advertising data

Free-tier use of the App shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob collects your advertising ID and device/usage signals to serve and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Google settings.

Crash and diagnostic data

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and basic device diagnostics (device model, OS version, app version) to fix bugs. This does not include your scanned document content.

Information collected automatically (scanning)

When you scan a document, the App captures photos via your device camera (through Google's ML Kit Document Scanner) and processes them on-device to produce cropped, enhanced scans and PDFs. These files are stored in the App's private storage on your device and are not transmitted to us unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.

We do not collect location, contacts, or any data beyond what's described above. We do not sell your data to third parties.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

In Short: We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law.

  • To facilitate account creation and authentication and otherwise manage user accounts. We process your email and auth identifier so you can sign in and sync documents.

  • To process purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat and Google Play Billing process purchase history to manage your Pro entitlement.
  • To serve and measure advertisements. AdMob uses device identifiers and usage signals to display and measure ads in the free tier.
  • To fix bugs and improve stability. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports and device diagnostics.
  • To communicate with you (e.g., support requests, subscription confirmations).
  • To comply with legal obligations and defend our legal rights.

5. Do We Use Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies?

In Short: The App uses device identifiers (advertising ID) for ad serving and crash diagnostics.

The App does not use browser cookies. It relies on your device's advertising ID (resettable in Google settings) for ad personalization and measurement, and on Crashlytics device identifiers for crash grouping.

Our website at orbitapps.co.ke may use minimal essential cookies for site navigation and security. Where analytics tools are enabled on the website, data is anonymized. You can adjust your browser settings to reject non-essential cookies.

8. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

In Short: We aim to protect your personal information through organizational and technical security measures.

  • Each account's documents and files are isolated at the database and storage level — your data is not accessible to other users.
  • Your authentication session is stored encrypted on your device.
  • Scanned documents and your session are excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.
  • Data is encrypted in transit between the App and our backend providers.

Despite our safeguards, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. Transmission of personal information to and from our Services is at your own risk.

11. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature. No uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized, so we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings regardless of DNT.

Global Privacy Control (GPC): We recognize and honor GPC signals where applicable under US state privacy laws. When we detect a GPC signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.
