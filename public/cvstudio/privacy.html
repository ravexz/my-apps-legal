1. What Information Do We Collect?

In Short: We do not collect personal information. CV Studio works entirely offline and on your device.

CV Studio is designed with privacy at its core. The App does not require an account, does not display advertisements, and does not include analytics or tracking SDKs. All app data — resumes, cover letters, job applications, interview notes, templates, your settings, and JSON backups — is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

Personal information you disclose to us

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the App. If you contact us voluntarily by email (for example, to ask a question or report a bug), we will process your name and email address solely to respond to your inquiry.

Sensitive information

We do not process sensitive information.

Information collected automatically

The App itself does not collect usage data, device identifiers, or advertising IDs. It works offline and makes no network requests for analytics or advertising. Google Play may collect standard distribution and crash information (e.g., install counts, device model, OS version) as part of delivering the App to you; this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

The App does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files beyond what you choose to import or export within the App. We do not sell your data to third parties.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

In Short: We do not process your app data because it never leaves your device. We only process information you voluntarily send us by email.

We process your information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries. If you email us for support or feedback, we process your contact details to reply.
  • To comply with legal obligations. We may process information where necessary to comply with applicable law, cooperate with law enforcement, or defend our legal rights.

3. What Legal Bases Do We Rely On to Process Your Personal Information?

In Short: We only process your personal information when we have a valid legal reason to do so under applicable law.

If you are located in the EU or UK: The GDPR and UK GDPR require us to explain the legal bases we rely on. We may rely on:

  • Legal Obligations. Where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.

If you are located in Canada: We may process your information with your express or implied consent, or as otherwise permitted by applicable law. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

4. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

In Short: We do not share your information. Your CV Studio data never leaves your device.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your app data with any third party. We may share information only in the following limited situations:

  • Business Transfers. We may share or transfer your information in connection with, or during negotiations of, any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business to another company.
  • Legal Requirements. We may disclose information to regulatory authorities or law enforcement agencies when mandated by valid legal process or court order.

5. Do We Use Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies?

In Short: The App does not use cookies or tracking technologies. Our website uses minimal essential cookies.

CV Studio does not use browser cookies, device identifiers, advertising IDs, or tracking pixels. The App works offline and makes no network requests for tracking or analytics.

Our website at orbitapps.co.ke may use minimal essential cookies for site navigation and security. Where analytics tools are enabled on the website, data is anonymized. You can adjust your browser settings to reject non-essential cookies.

6. How Do We Handle Your Social Logins?

In Short: CV Studio does not offer social login or third-party authentication.

CV Studio does not require or support registration or login through Facebook, X, Google, or any other social media account. No cloud account is required.

7. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

In Short: Your app data stays on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. We do not retain your app data because we never receive it.

If you email us for support, we retain your correspondence only as long as necessary to address your inquiry and comply with legal obligations (typically no longer than six (6) months past the start of the idle period). You control the retention of your app data locally and may delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing your data within the App.

8. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

In Short: Your data stays on your device under your device's security. We do not maintain server-side personal data.

CV Studio does not transmit your resumes, cover letters, job applications, notes, or settings to any server. Those remain on your device under your device's security (screen lock, encryption). For support emails, we apply reasonable organizational measures. No electronic transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, and transmission is at your own risk.

10. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

In Short: Depending on where you are located, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information. Because we do not collect your app data, you exercise direct control over your data on your device.

In some regions (such as the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and Canada), you have certain rights under applicable data protection laws. Because CV Studio does not collect personal information directly from you, most of these rights will have limited application to us. Your app data is fully under your control on your device — you can export it as JSON backups, edit it, or delete it at any time. 11. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature. No uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized, so we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals. CV Studio does not track your online browsing activity in any event.

Global Privacy Control (GPC): We recognize and honor GPC signals where applicable under US state privacy laws. When we detect a GPC signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.
