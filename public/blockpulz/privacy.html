1. What Information Do We Collect?

In Short: BLOCKPULZ is offline-first. We do not collect personal information.

BLOCKPULZ is designed with privacy at its core. The App does not require an account, does not display advertisements, and does not include analytics or tracking SDKs. All game data — scores, high scores, level progress and stars, achievements, in-game coins, unlocked skins and themes, daily-challenge streak, your sound and vibration settings, and the state of an unfinished game — is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

Personal information you disclose to us

We do not collect personal information that you provide to us. If you contact us voluntarily by email (for example, to ask a question or report a bug), we will process your name and email address solely to respond to your inquiry.

Sensitive information

We do not process sensitive information.

Information collected automatically

The App itself does not collect usage data, device identifiers, or advertising IDs. Google Play may collect standard distribution and crash information (e.g., install counts, device model, OS version) as part of delivering the App to you; this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

In Short: We process information only to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law.
